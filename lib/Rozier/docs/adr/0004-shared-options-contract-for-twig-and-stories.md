# Twig components and stories share one options contract

A component's markup is produced twice: by its Twig template in `lib/RoadizRozierBundle` for the backoffice, and by a TypeScript renderer (`app/utils/component-renderer/`, `app/utils/storybook/renderer/`) for its story, since Storybook has no PHP runtime. The two drift apart. The goal is a single, synchronised rendering, but the mechanism (a Symfony rendering endpoint fetched by Storybook, HTML fixtures generated by Symfony, or Twig run in the browser) and its cost are not known yet, so that choice is deferred to a spike and may be postponed. What we decide now is the contract every mechanism needs: a component's Twig `options` and its renderer's `Options` type have the same keys and meaning.

## Consequences

- Renaming the Twig option keys is part of the v2.8 template move ([ADR 0005](0005-twig-components-move-in-v2-8.md)).
- Until the sync mechanism ships, renderers remain the source of story markup, and a PR that changes the markup or options of an `rz-*` root on one side updates the other in the same PR; `/code-review` checks it against [`storybook.md`](../storybook.md) and [`templates.md`](../templates.md).
- Picking a sync mechanism is a new ADR; it does not supersede this one.
